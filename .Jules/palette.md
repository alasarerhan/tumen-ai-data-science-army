## 2024-05-20 - [Add Copy-to-Clipboard to Code Artifacts]
**Learning:** Providing immediate visual and interaction feedback (icon change + toast) for copy actions significantly improves perceived reliability of the UI. Ensuring ARIA labels are present on icon-only buttons is crucial for screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** Always include a visual confirmation state (like a checkmark) and a toast notification for clipboard operations. Ensure icon-only buttons have descriptive aria-labels.
